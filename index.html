Privacy Policy

Sumello – Math & Brain Games

Effective Date: November 13, 2026
Last Updated: November 13, 2026

Welcome to Sumello – Math & Brain Games ("Sumello", "we", "our", or "us"). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile application.

Sumello is a math and brain-training game designed to help users practice mental calculations, improve number skills, solve puzzles, and enjoy educational games.

1. Information We Collect

Sumello does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, email address, telephone number, or home address to play the game.

We do not operate a backend server or a developer-operated personal information collection system for the current version of the application.

Depending on the features you use, the app may store certain gameplay information locally on your device, such as:

  • Game scores and achievements.
  • Game progress and completed levels.
  • Coins, points, or other in-game progress.
  • Game preferences and settings.
  • Sound and other gameplay preferences.

This information is used to support gameplay and improve your experience. The exact information stored depends on the features implemented in your installed version.

2. How We Use Information

Any gameplay information stored locally may be used to:

  • Save your game progress.
  • Display scores and achievements.
  • Remember your preferences and settings.
  • Provide core game functionality.
  • Improve the overall user experience.

We do not sell users' personal information.

3. Data Storage and Security

Gameplay information stored locally remains on your device unless a future feature or third-party service changes this arrangement.

You may be able to remove locally stored app information by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the application. Uninstalling the app may permanently delete locally stored gameplay progress.

We take reasonable steps in designing and maintaining the app to avoid unnecessary collection of personal information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

4. Permissions Used by the App

Sumello may use device capabilities required for its functionality. The permissions requested by the installed version should be reviewed in your device settings.

The current project configuration includes audio-related Android permissions. These should only be retained when required by an actual app feature.

Sumello does not intend to record conversations or access microphone input for gameplay in the current version. If microphone access is introduced in a future version, this policy will be updated to explain its purpose and use.

5. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The current version of Sumello does not have Google AdMob advertising enabled, and we do not currently use Firebase Analytics or a user authentication service.

If advertising or other third-party services are introduced in a future version, those services may process certain information in accordance with their own privacy policies and the app's configuration.

For example, if Google Mobile Ads is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers and device information.
  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it.
  • Ad impressions, clicks, and interaction information.
  • Diagnostics and advertising performance information.

These examples describe possible processing if advertising is added; they are not a statement that the current version collects this information.

Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Children's Privacy

Sumello is a math and brain-training game that may be useful to users of different age groups.

The current version does not require account registration or ask users to submit personal information to play.

If future versions introduce advertising, analytics, accounts, or other services, we will review their data practices and update the app's disclosures and settings as required by applicable laws and Google Play policies, including requirements that apply to children.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties. The current version does not send gameplay data to a developer-operated backend server.

If third-party services are added in the future, information may be processed by those providers as necessary for the relevant feature. This policy will be updated accordingly.

8. Your Privacy Choices

You can manage your device's app permissions through Android settings. You may also clear the app's stored data or uninstall Sumello if you no longer wish to use it.

Please note that clearing app data or uninstalling the app may remove locally stored game progress and preferences.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application changes, new features are introduced, or legal and platform requirements change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
